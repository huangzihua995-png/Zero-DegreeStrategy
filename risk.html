UNDERSTANDING MARKET RISK

Key Risk Considerations

Digital asset markets involve unique risks. Understanding these factors can help individuals and organizations make more informed decisions.

01 / MARKET VOLATILITY

Price Fluctuations

Digital asset prices can change rapidly. Market sentiment, liquidity, economic developments, and regulatory changes may all influence price movements.

02 / LIQUIDITY

Market Liquidity Risk

Trading volume and market depth can vary significantly. Limited liquidity may affect execution prices and the ability to enter or exit positions.

03 / LEVERAGE

Leverage and Derivatives

Leveraged products and derivatives can amplify both potential gains and losses. Understanding margin requirements and liquidation mechanisms is important.

04 / COUNTERPARTY

Platform and Counterparty Exposure

Digital asset participants may face risks involving exchanges, custodians, service providers, and other counterparties.

05 / TECHNOLOGY

Operational and Technology Risk

Cybersecurity incidents, software vulnerabilities, smart contract failures, and operational disruptions can affect digital asset activities.

06 / REGULATORY

Regulatory Developments

Changes in laws, regulations, and compliance requirements may influence digital asset markets, products, and business operations.

Our Perspective

Risk cannot be eliminated, but it can be better understood. We emphasize research, careful evaluation, and informed decision-making when examining digital asset opportunities and challenges.
