Mission & Positioning

Zero Degree Strategy is an engineering and research group centered on microstructural analysis, BTC/USDT options quantitative modeling, and dedicated trading node deployments. We operate strictly by our core principle: Strategy is Code, Risk is Absolute.

Our focus is anchored strictly in mathematical finance and distributed systems architecture. We do not operate public investment funds, nor do we provide third-party asset custody or retail wealth management. Our goal is to empower institutional and research partners with transparent, resilient, and verifiable execution infrastructure.
